✦ Deploy everywhere

A CREATE2 address is the same on every chain that has your deployer. Before you send anything, this checks whether it is still free on each of them, because somebody else's deployment can already occupy your address on one chain while it is untouched on the rest, and finding that out after the fact is expensive.

2. Deploy

The Arachnid proxy takes salt ‖ initCode as raw calldata with no function selector, so any wallet can send it. Connect, pick a chain, and this builds the transaction. Nothing is signed without your explicit confirmation in the wallet.
