✦ Verify

Two checks, both entirely in this tab. The first needs no signature and no trust: given a deployer, a salt and an init-code hash, the address is one keccak away, so a claim about a CREATE2 address is either true or provably false.

Re-derive an address

Verify a grind attestation

An attestation adds provenance to a derivation: who ground the salt, when, and how many attempts it took. Verification recomputes the address, the pattern and the difficulty, then recovers the signer and checks it against the issuer list at /.well-known/create2-vanity.json.

Checking it on chain

The attestation is EIP-712 typed data, so a contract recovers the signer with ecrecover. A registry can then require a valid attestation before recording an address as deliberately ground. 

bytes32 constant TYPE_HASH = keccak256(
    "Create2Attestation(address account,address deployer,bytes32 salt,bytes32 initCodeHash,"
    "string prefix,string suffix,bool caseSensitive,uint256 expectedAttempts,"
    "uint256 attempts,bytes32 nonce,uint256 issuedAt)"
);

// The derivation needs no signature at all:
address predicted = address(uint160(uint256(keccak256(
    abi.encodePacked(bytes1(0xff), deployer, salt, initCodeHash)
))));
require(predicted == a.account, "salt does not produce this address");
